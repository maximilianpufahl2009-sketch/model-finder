How the quality score works

The score runs from 0 to 100 and is the sum of four parts. Click a score on any card to see its breakdown.

  • Popularity (up to 40): likes on Sketchfab, bookmarks on Blendkit, downloads on Poly Haven, on a log scale. 5,000 likes, 1,000 bookmarks or 100,000 downloads gets full marks.
  • Like rate or rating (up to 15): likes ÷ views on Sketchfab (5% or more gets full marks). On Blendkit, the 1–10 quality rating from reviewers, counted fully once 3 people have rated. Poly Haven publishes neither, so it gets a neutral 10.
  • Curated (up to 20): a Sketchfab Staff Pick (20), any Poly Haven model (20, every one is made by their team), or any Blendkit model (10, every upload is checked by moderators).
  • Craft (up to 25): texture resolution (up to 15), a full texture set of 3 or more maps (5), and a written description (5).

The polygon budget, animated and rigged filters are applied by Sketchfab and Blendkit during the search, so you get a full page of matching models instead of a filtered-down handful. Poly Haven has no animated or rigged models, so it drops out when you tick either box.

This is a rule of thumb built on public signals. It can't see topology, UVs or scale. Open the 3D preview before you buy anything.

Licenses are read from each site's own data. Check the license on the model page before you use a model in a commercial project.
